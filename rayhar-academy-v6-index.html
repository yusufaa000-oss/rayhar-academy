About RAYHAR ACADEMY

Our Mission

RAYHAR ACADEMY is committed to providing free, accessible, quality education that combines rigorous academic foundations with practical, real-world application. We empower students to become independent thinkers, skilled practitioners, and responsible leaders through theory-driven learning, direct mentorship, and hands-on projects.

Our Vision

We envision a world where education is a universal right, not a privilege. RAYHAR ACADEMY aspires to develop informed, capable, and ethical leaders across diverse academic disciplines who can contribute meaningfully to their communities and society at large, regardless of their geographic location or economic background.

Our Goals & Objectives

Democratize Education

Foster Leadership

Academic Overview

Transformative Education Without Barriers

At RAYHAR ACADEMY, we believe that transformative education should be accessible to everyone, regardless of background or geography. We are committed to providing free, theory-driven, and mentor-guided learning that equips students with not only technical knowledge, but also the critical thinking, creativity, and leadership skills needed to excel in a rapidly changing world.

Our approach combines rigorous academic foundations, hands-on project work, and direct mentorship. We welcome complete beginners seeking to build new skills, professionals looking to advance their careers, and career-changers ready to pursue new opportunities—all without unnecessary barriers to access.

Whether you are beginning your learning journey or refining existing expertise, RAYHAR ACADEMY aims to meet you where you are and provide the guidance, knowledge, and support needed to progress toward your goals.

Learning Beyond the Classroom

Education at RAYHAR ACADEMY extends beyond theoretical knowledge. Through mentorship, practical projects, collaborative learning, and engagement with real-world challenges, students are encouraged to apply what they learn and develop the confidence to solve problems creatively and responsibly.

Developing Leaders for a Changing World

Beyond technical mastery, we are dedicated to developing global leaders across diverse academic disciplines. Our educational approach seeks to cultivate expertise alongside integrity, critical thinking, creativity, responsibility, and a commitment to meaningful contribution to society.

Through theory, mentorship, and real-world projects, RAYHAR ACADEMY seeks to prepare students to lead with knowledge, serve with integrity, and contribute with purpose in their chosen fields.

Academic Calendar & Flexible Admission

Flexible Admission

RAYHAR ACADEMY operates with a flexible admission system. Students can apply at any time throughout the year, and there is no fixed admission period. There is also no admission fee, allowing students to begin their application without an additional financial barrier.

Academic Schedule

Academic activities are organized according to the schedule of each class or program. Students receive information about their classes, assignments, examinations, and other academic activities through the academy's learning channels. No fixed holiday schedule is currently established.

Assessment & Certification

RAYHAR ACADEMY combines continuous learning with academic assessment to help students demonstrate their understanding and progress.

Students may participate in:

  • Assignments
  • Class participation
  • Practical/project-based activities
  • Examinations
  • Student–teacher interaction
  • Other academic assessments relevant to their studies

Certification

Students who successfully complete the requirements of their respective program or course will be eligible to receive a RAYHAR ACADEMY certificate in accordance with the academy's certification requirements.

Online Learning & Academic Experience

RAYHAR ACADEMY provides its education primarily through online learning, making it possible for students to participate regardless of their location.

Live Classes

Students participate in live online classes, primarily through Google Meet, allowing direct interaction between teachers and students.

Recorded Lessons

Recorded lessons will also be made available to students, providing an opportunity to review lessons and study at their own pace.

Assignments

Students receive assignments to reinforce their understanding and apply what they learn.

Student–Teacher Interaction

Direct interaction between students and teachers is an important part of the learning experience, allowing students to ask questions, receive guidance, and participate actively in class.

Study Materials

Students receive relevant study materials to support their learning and academic preparation.

Examinations

Students will participate in examinations as part of the academy's assessment process.
