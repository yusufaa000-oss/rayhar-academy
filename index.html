About RAYHAR ACADEMY

Education With Purpose

RAYHAR ACADEMY is built around accessible learning, knowledge, practical development, mentorship and leadership.

Our Mission

To make meaningful education accessible to learners from diverse backgrounds while encouraging knowledge, critical thinking, practical skills, integrity and leadership.

Our Vision

To contribute to a world where access to quality learning is not unnecessarily limited by geography or background, and where educated and capable people can contribute positively to society.

What We Value

  • Knowledge
  • Critical Thinking
  • Practical Learning
  • Integrity
  • Leadership
  • Lifelong Learning

Who Can Apply?

RAYHAR ACADEMY welcomes learners interested in developing their knowledge and skills through the available departments and learning opportunities.
