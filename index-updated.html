About RAYHAR ACADEMY

Education With Purpose

Accessible online education focused on knowledge, practical learning, mentorship, critical thinking and leadership.

Our Mission

To make meaningful education accessible to learners from diverse backgrounds by providing free, mentor-guided online learning that develops knowledge, practical abilities, critical thinking, creativity, integrity and leadership.

Our Vision

To contribute to a world where access to quality learning is not limited by geography or background, and where educated, ethical and capable people use knowledge to create positive change in their communities and beyond.

Our Goals & Objectives

  • Expand access to learning without unnecessary financial barriers.
  • Support students through mentorship and meaningful teacher interaction.
  • Connect academic theory with practical projects and real-world challenges.
  • Encourage critical thinking, creativity, responsibility and integrity.
  • Develop learners who can contribute positively to society and lead with purpose.

Who We Welcome

RAYHAR ACADEMY welcomes beginners, students, learners developing existing knowledge, and people seeking new academic or professional opportunities. Admission is open and flexible, subject to the academy's learning arrangements.

Academic

Transformative Education Without Barriers

RAYHAR ACADEMY combines academic foundations, practical projects, mentorship and online learning to support students at different stages.

Learning Beyond the Classroom

Education at RAYHAR ACADEMY extends beyond theoretical knowledge. Through mentorship, practical projects, collaborative learning, and engagement with real-world challenges, students are encouraged to apply what they learn and develop the confidence to solve problems creatively and responsibly.

Developing Leaders for a Changing World

Beyond technical mastery, RAYHAR ACADEMY seeks to develop global leaders across diverse academic disciplines by cultivating expertise alongside integrity, critical thinking, creativity, responsibility, and a commitment to meaningful contribution to society.

Flexible Admission & Academic Calendar

Admission is open and flexible. Students can apply at any time. There is no admission fee.

Academic activities are organized according to the schedule of each class or program. Students receive information about classes, assignments, examinations and other academic activities through the academy's learning channels.

Holiday schedule: No fixed holiday schedule is currently established.

Assessment & Certification

Students may participate in assignments, class participation, practical or project-based activities, student-teacher interaction and examinations as part of their learning and assessment.

Students who successfully complete the requirements of their respective course or program will be eligible to receive a RAYHAR ACADEMY certificate in accordance with the academy's certification requirements.

Online Learning Experience

  • Live classes: Online video classes, primarily through Google Meet.
  • Recorded lessons: Lessons will also be made available to students for review and self-paced study.
  • Assignments: Learning activities designed to reinforce understanding.
  • Student-teacher interaction: Direct communication, questions and guidance.
  • Study materials: Relevant materials to support academic preparation.
  • Examinations: Exams form part of the assessment process.
